# ForgeGov v2.6.1 — Collaboration & Stability

- Company Hub centralizes company profile, team members, roles, invitations, and partners.
- Employee membership is invitation-bound; company roles remain separate from job titles.
- Invitation lifecycle supports delivery status, resend, cancellation, expiration, and audit history.
- Transactional email infrastructure uses Django's configured email backend and fails safely without losing the in-app invitation.
- In-app notification center covers membership and Project Room collaboration events.
- Project Room partner invitations now notify recipient-company members and log activity.
- Pipeline workspace links are generated by the backend from canonical opportunity source IDs.
- Responsive corrections improve Company Hub and notification workflows on tablet and mobile.
